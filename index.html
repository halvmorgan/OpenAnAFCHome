Questions

I don't live in Michigan. Is it still useful?

Yes, with one adjustment. Every item is tagged Michigan-specific or universal. The universal parts (cost planning, daily operations, filling beds, staffing, complaints) apply anywhere. The Michigan parts tell you exactly what to look up for your own state.

How long does licensing take?

Plan on 6 months to a year from your first call to LARA to a license in hand. The inspection scheduling wait is usually the biggest delay. The Playbook covers what slows people down and how to avoid the self-inflicted delays.

Do I need a healthcare background?

No medical license is required. Under the current rules, the licensee needs a high school diploma or GED and at least 1 year of direct experience with the population you plan to serve, plus background checks, a medical clearance and health screening. You also need to be ready for hands-on care.

Will this tell me how much I'll make?

No one can honestly promise a number. The spreadsheet helps you model your own home, including a slow first few months, so you know your real break-even point before you open.
